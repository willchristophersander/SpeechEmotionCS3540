1. CREMA-D Dataset

Cao, H., Cooper, D. G., Keutmann, M. K., Gur, R. C., Nenkova, A., & Verma, R. (2014). CREMA-D: Crowd-sourced emotional multimodal actors dataset. IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing, 5(4), 377–390. Available at GitHub

2. EmoDB Dataset

Burkhardt, F., Paeschke, A., Rolfes, M., Sendlmeier, W. F., & Weiss, B. (2005). A database of German emotional speech. Proceedings of Interspeech, 1517–1520.

3. RAVDESS Dataset

Livingstone, S. R., & Russo, F. A. (2018). The Ryerson Audio-Visual Database of Emotional Speech and Song (RAVDESS): A dynamic, multimodal set of facial and vocal expressions in North American English. PloS One, 13(5), e0196391. Available at Zenodo

4. SAVEE Dataset

Jackson, P., & Haq, S. (2008). Surrey Audio-Visual Expressed Emotion (SAVEE) Database. University of Surrey, UK. Official site

5. TESS Dataset

Pichora-Fuller, M. K., Dupuis, K., & Reed, M. (2020). Toronto Emotional Speech Set (TESS). University of Toronto, Psychology Department.

6. IEMOCAP Dataset

Busso, C., Bulut, M., Lee, C.-C., Kazemzadeh, A., Mower, E., Kim, S., … & Narayanan, S. S. (2008). IEMOCAP: Interactive emotional dyadic motion capture database. Language Resources and Evaluation, 42(4), 335–359. Official access page

7. nEMO Dataset

Spapé, M., Mäkelä, K., & Ruotsalo, T. (2024). NEMO: A Database for Emotion Analysis Using Functional Near-Infrared Spectroscopy. IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing, 15(3), 1166-1177. doi: 10.1109/TAFFC.2023.3315971

8. MELD Dataset

Poria, S., Hazarika, D., Majumder, N., Naik, G., Cambria, E., & Mihalcea, R. (2019). MELD: A Multimodal Multi-Party Dataset for Emotion Recognition in Conversations. Proceedings of the 57th Annual Meeting of the Association for Computational Linguistics, 527–536. Project page

9. SpecAugment

Park, D. S., Chan, W., Zhang, Y., Chiu, C.-C., Zoph, B., Cubuk, E. D., & Le, Q. V. (2019). SpecAugment: A simple data augmentation method for automatic speech recognition. Interspeech, 2613–2617. arXiv preprint

10. CRNN Architectures

Graves, A., & Schmidhuber, J. (2005). Framewise phoneme classification with bidirectional LSTM and other neural network architectures. Neural Networks, 18(5–6), 602–610.

11. Attention Mechanisms

Bahdanau, D., Cho, K., & Bengio, Y. (2014). Neural machine translation by jointly learning to align and translate. arXiv preprint arXiv:1409.0473.

Vaswani, A., Shazeer, N., Parmar, N., Uszkoreit, J., Jones, L., Gomez, A. N., … & Polosukhin, I. (2017). Attention is All You Need. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems, 30.

12. Speech Emotion Recognition Survey

El Ayadi, M., Kamel, M. S., & Karray, F. (2011). Survey on speech emotion recognition: Features, classification schemes, and databases. Pattern Recognition, 44(3), 572–587.

Khalil, R. A., Jones, E., Babar, M. I., Jan, T., Zafar, M. H., & Alhussain, T. (2019). Speech emotion recognition using deep learning techniques: A review. IEEE Access, 7, 117327–117345.

13. Librosa Library

McFee, B., Raffel, C., Liang, D., Ellis, D. P., McVicar, M., Battenberg, E., & Nieto, O. (2015). librosa: Audio and music analysis in Python. Proceedings of the 14th Python in Science Conference, 18–25. Library website

14. PyTorch

Paszke, A., Gross, S., Massa, F., Lerer, A., Bradbury, J., Chanan, G., … & Chintala, S. (2019). PyTorch: An imperative style, high-performance deep learning library. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems, 32. Official site
